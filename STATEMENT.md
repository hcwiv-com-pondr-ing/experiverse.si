# Henry C. Weismann IV: statement and signature (draft, 2026-11-05)

Henry C. Weismann IV is a software developer and systems designer, the owner of weismannweb.com, and the founder of
New Earth Network. After more than a decade building software for clients, he now works on one question: in an age
when software can speak, write and act like a person, how do people prove they are human, and that what they know is
real, without giving up who they are?

## The work

**First, a proof of humanness for the whole internet.** Henry is building a small open-source application that,
paired with a hardware security key (a YubiKey or any FIDO2 key), proves a real person is present behind any action,
on any device, without revealing who that person is. Each person is known only by a pseudonym. From that proof grows a
chain of activity, from the human to their devices to everything they create online, so that any digital work can
show it came from a person. The design rests on open standards that already exist (FIDO2 and WebAuthn, W3C Verifiable
Credentials, IPsec, Tor), and it is being written up as an open standard of its own: the Humanness Standard, published at
[purehuman.io](https://purehuman.io). It comes as two separate open projects. **ExperiVerse Pure Human** authenticates:
it signs a person in and proves they are human. **Humanproof** ([humanproof.me](https://humanproof.me)) verifies: anyone
can check a proof, and it plugs into the authentication project and its chain without ever holding its keys.

**Second, merit without identity.** On top of that proof sits a merit-based, quadratic voting system recorded on a
chain. Merit is proven the way a notary proves a signature: by documents certified to modern W3C verifiable-credential
standards, by background and credit checks through established providers, or by other attestations. The system
judges nothing. It records only that something happened: a degree, a career, years of poverty, twenty years of
addiction and the road out of it. Every kind of life experience counts as a form of knowledge, whatever society thinks
of it. Your proofs speak; your name never has to.

**Third, a more conscious consensus reality.** The larger product will live at consensusreality.org. Its first piece
is a browser extension that overlays the whole web, so anyone can comment on any page with merit-weighted comments and
votes. A comment shows no identity, only the commenter's signature and the chain of merit that relates to that topic
and that part of the web, and nothing beyond what proves their standing to speak there. The site will publish big-data
reports on the conversations happening across this federated, IPsec-protected record, stored on Tor and peer-to-peer
networks, showing where proven experience agrees and where it does not.

**Fourth, a shared intelligence of agents and people.** [experiverse.si](https://experiverse.si) is the home of the
agentic side of this work. Software agents are fast and tireless; people bring judgment, lived experience and care.
Joined under the same proofs, they can form a super intelligence that is answerable to the humans in it rather than
apart from them. At its center is a large shared data set that grows only by permission: each person decides, item by
item, what they share, with whom and for how long, and can take it back. Contributions carry the same merit chain as
everything else, so the data set knows how much proven experience stands behind each piece without knowing whose it
is. The aims are practical: better health, a clearer understanding of our worlds both small and large, better
technology, and better ways of governing and of helping society grow. Agents act inside each person's scope, report
what they find, and never act as the person.

## Why it matters

Captchas no longer tell people from machines, and the web rewards whoever is loudest, not whoever knows. Proof of
personhood, verifiable credentials and quadratic voting each exist today as separate pieces. Henry's work joins them
under one rule: prove the human, prove the experience, protect the person. The aim is a shared picture of reality
built consciously, by people who can show what they have lived, rather than the default one we drift in now.

## How the initiatives fit together

All of Henry's work starts at [aquariusintuitive.com](https://aquariusintuitive.com), his home for philosophy,
sociology, epistemology and the technology that serves them, and for his Labs, where physical products and community
work take shape. **The Tsiyon Grid** ([tsiyongrid.com](https://tsiyongrid.com)) is the home of the whole ecosystem and
his systems design for cities and small towns facing addiction, poverty, mental health, housing and food insecurity;
**New Earth Network**, gig work for good, is its worker side. **ExperiVerse Pure Human** ([purehuman.io](https://purehuman.io)), his
Experience Engine, gives people ownership of their own data and credentials. The **Humanness Standard** is the proof all of them stand on, and
**Consensus Reality** ([consensusreality.org](https://consensusreality.org)) is where they meet the wider web.
**ExperiVerse SI** ([experiverse.si](https://experiverse.si)) is where people and agents think together.

## This signature

This statement sits at the root of every repository Henry keeps, public or private, or wherever a repository's public
part lives. Where he signs something public, it carries this signature and his proof of humanness: a signature made
with his own hardware key, which anyone can check at humanproof.me, so a reader knows the work came from him and that
a human was present when he signed it.
