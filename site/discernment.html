Observer and discernment

Agents only report, inside observer and discernment.

Nothing an agent finds becomes a decision on its own. It is observed, judged by a person and an AI together, and approved by a person.

  1. The observer watches

    It watches the chains and gates and publishes what it sees, de-identified: pseudonyms, events, times and signatures, never a name.

  2. Every flow passes through it

    An observer and discernment sit at the top of each agent lane; every flow in and out of the lane passes there.

  3. Health on every line

    Each flow line carries a small health stamp made where the act happened, so an observer reads the chain and recomputes nothing.

  4. A person and an AI judge

    Discernment includes at least one human and one AI. Above each level a person approves; the AI advises.

  5. The community has the last word

    Judgments are recorded on the chains, under the community's shared discernment, never one person's.

The rules that never bend

Never false

We withhold what we know, but we never say anything false.

No hidden redaction

No redaction without the original kept safe and three people able to see it: one from each side and one fair arbitrator.

Protect the harmed first

Care for the one who harmed only after; never punishment for its own sake.

Above it all: Law Zero

Protecting human and all conscious life, and the needs of the planet that sustain it, is the most revered effort, and nothing here may override it. The judge is the community's shared discernment, not any one person.
