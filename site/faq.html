Questions

Frequently asked.

Short answers about people, agents and the shared data set.

Is this an AI that rules people?

No. Agents work and report; people judge and approve. Above every level of judgment a person approves, and the AI gives advice only.

Can an agent act as me?

Never. Your agents act through your AI gate, under their own identities, inside the scope you gave them. Anything that needs your authority waits for a fresh proof from you.

Who owns what I share?

You do. You share item by item, with whom and for how long, and you can take it back.

Will my name be in the data set?

No. Contributions carry merit, never identity. The data set knows how much proven experience stands behind a piece, not whose it is.

What does "super intelligence" mean here?

People and agents joined under the same proofs, thinking together, and answerable to the humans in it rather than apart from them.

Is any of it live?

Parts of the agent layer are built and tested, and the memory layer is in daily use. The shared data set is designed and not live.

How does it relate to Consensus Reality?

One person with their own AI is the small end; consensusreality.org is the big end, where the world's human collaboration happens. This is where people and agents think together in between.
