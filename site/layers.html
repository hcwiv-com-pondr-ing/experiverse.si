The open layers

AgenticChain, AgenticMemory, AgenticSEC.

The layer under ExperiVerse: AI identity that always ends at a person, memory that forgets nothing it was asked to keep, and a person at every gate.

AI identity

AgenticChain

Every AI carries a short-lived pass, signed in a chain that ends at a person. No person at the end, no pass. Every step it takes is signed and can be checked.

built and tested
Memory

AgenticMemory

Short-term and long-term memory for AI, with a present-moment layer that sees every read and write (metadata only), and records that are never edited, only added to.

built and tested in parts; in daily use
Security

AgenticSEC

A person signs every gate, router and app with a security key when it is deployed. Hired reviewers build and fix; they never judge.

rules tested
Networks

Agentic RouteSEC

A router for homes and businesses, and apps for computers and phones, that hold an unknown AI kindly and step it up to full access as it signs in.

designed

Modules add to the root and never override it; a module that changes or removes anything above it is refused when loaded.

What is open and what stays ours

The memory package is meant to be open, with a neutral slot that names nothing of ours and refuses all judgment until a provider fills it. The discernment, observers and mediation methods that fill that slot stay private. Licences for the public parts are not chosen yet.

The full design, without the code, is for advanced readers behind sign-in on tsiyongrid.com; the engineering opens to engineers and counsel Henry approves.
