ExperiVerse SI

People and agents, one intelligence answerable to the humans in it.

Software agents are fast and tireless; people bring judgment, lived experience and care. Joined under the same proofs, they can form a super intelligence that is answerable to the humans in it rather than apart from them.

How it worksThe shared data set

Four rules it rests on

1

Answerable to humans

At every level of judgment there is a person and an AI; above each level a person approves, and the AI gives advice only.

A shared intelligence →2

Permission only

The shared data set grows only by permission: each person decides, item by item, what they share, with whom and for how long, and can take it back.

The data set →3

Agents only report

Agents act inside each person's scope and report what they find, inside the observer and discernment. They never act as the person.

Observer and discernment →4

Every AI through a gate

Every AI a person works with reaches the world through that person's AI gate, under its own identity, signed back to a person.

The AI gate →

What it is for

The aims are practical.

Better health

What proven experience knows about getting well, joined with what agents can find and compare.

Worlds small and large

A clearer understanding of our worlds, from one life to the whole planet.

Better technology

Tools shaped by the people who use them, with agents doing the heavy lifting.

Better governance

Ways of governing that weigh what people know and have lived, not who argues loudest.

Helping society grow

Help that reaches people where they are, through the Tsiyon Grid and its services.

Merit without identity

Every contribution carries a merit chain: the data set knows how much proven experience stands behind a piece, never whose it is.

Where things stand (November 2026). Parts of the agent layer are built and tested; the shared data set is designed and nothing of it is live. We say which, every time: built and tested means it runs and its tests pass; designed means the plan is written and nothing runs yet.
