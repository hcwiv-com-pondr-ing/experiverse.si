The shared data set

It grows only by permission.

At the center is a large shared data set for health, understanding, technology, governance and helping society grow. Each person decides, item by item, what goes in.

Your permission, item by item

  1. What

    You choose each item you share. Nothing is taken by default.

  2. With whom

    You choose who may use it: the whole data set, a group, or one study.

  3. For how long

    Every permission has an end date.

  4. Take it back

    You can withdraw an item; it leaves what is built next.

Merit, not identity

Contributions carry the same merit chain as everything else in this ecosystem. Merit is proven the way a notary proves a signature, by certified documents and attestations, and records only that something happened: a degree, a career, years of poverty, twenty years of addiction and the road out of it. Every kind of life experience counts as a form of knowledge. The data set knows how much proven experience stands behind each piece without knowing whose it is.

Safeguards, as designed

Raw rows stay home

Raw records never leave the person's side. What leaves is aggregates.

Mathematical privacy

Aggregates are released with differential privacy, so what any one person adds is bounded.

No tiny groups

Groups under a size floor are suppressed; social graphs can re-identify people from a few links.

Synthetic data

Others work with synthetic data made under the same guarantee, never with real rows.

Others in a chat agreed too

Nothing of another person's is scored without their own yes.

Labels are guesses

A group label from a model is a guess with a confidence, never a verdict on a person.

Designed, not live. The data set is a plan. It is built first in a sandbox with synthetic people and agents, so every method is tested before any real record is read.
