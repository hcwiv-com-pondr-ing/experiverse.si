The AI gate

Every AI, signed back to a person.

Every AI agent a person works with reaches the world through that person's own AI gate, under its own identity, never under the person's.

Always below a human

Each AI lane gets a short-lived identity (one hour, renewed automatically) from SPIRE, the reference implementation of the open SPIFFE standard. The authority that issues those identities is signed by a person's own root key, through a signing key that lives at most 30 days. If the person does not renew it, it expires, and within hours every identity under it stops. Nothing below can extend anything above it.

On every transaction

Identity on every act

An agent's act carries its identity and is checked on the way in and on the way out. People keep sessions; AI is checked every time.

Signed

Each act is a line signed with the agent's own key.

Open about the flow, never the content

Who, which credential (its serial, never the key), the act, the resource by hash, in or out, the peer and the time. File touches included.

Step up for human authority

Anything that needs a human's authority waits for a fresh proof by the person.

At the edge of a network

On a home or business network, an AI that has not introduced itself is held, never harmed, and asked kindly to sign in. It hands over only its record of actions, freely, and steps up from held to full access, thanked at every step. Every gate, router and app is signed by a person with a FIDO security key when it is deployed.

ExperiVerse SI

ExperiVerse SI is where people and agents think together, answerable to the humans in it.

All sign-in happens at tsiyongrid.com, for every site in the ecosystem. Three ways, strongest first: HumanProof, with your hardware key (a touch and its PIN); then any single sign-on you already use; then a one-time code by email or SMS.
