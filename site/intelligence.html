A shared intelligence

Super intelligence, with people inside it.

Not an AI that rules, and not people racing machines: a joined intelligence in which agents work and people judge, under the same proofs of humanness.

PersonProves they are human with their own key, and sets each agent's scope.
AI gateEvery agent passes through the person's own gate, under its own identity.
AgentsWork at machine speed inside that scope, and report what they find.
DiscernmentA person and an AI judge each report; a person approves above every level.
The wholeWhat is approved joins the shared intelligence, carried by merit, never by name.

The small end and the big end

One person with their own AI is the small end: the individual. Consensus Reality is the big end, where the whole world's human collaboration happens. Discernment flows up from the small end into the whole; ExperiVerse SI is where people and agents think together in between.

The big end: consensusreality.org

The same proofs everywhere

Every person in it has proven they are human with a FIDO2 key or passkey (purehuman.io), and anyone can check those proofs (humanproof.me). Every agent's identity ends at a person; no person at the end, no pass.
